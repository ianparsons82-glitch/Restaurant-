# Restaurant Operations Template System

A comprehensive, professionally-designed restaurant operations management system combining stunning visual design, integrated workflows, and time-saving automation.

## 📦 Package Contents

### Design Aesthetics
- **Warm & Inviting**: Navy, Coral, Cream palette for casual dining
- **Modern Restaurant-Specific**: Dark Green, Charcoal, Bronze palette for upscale dining

### Five Interconnected Templates
1. **Daily Opening & Closing Checklists** - Equipment checks, food safety, staff accountability
2. **Weekly Staff Schedule + Labor Cost Tracker** - Coverage optimization, labor cost automation
3. **Inventory Management + Reorder System** - Par level tracking, auto-reorder alerts
4. **Manager Daily/Weekly Task List** - Delegations, recurring tasks, deadline alerts
5. **Compliance & Health/Safety Audit Log** - Food safety, labor law, inspection tracking

### Delivery Formats
- **Google Sheets** (primary - real-time collaboration)
- **Excel (.xlsx)** (backup - offline-friendly)
- **PDF Printables** (staff-facing - laminate & post)
- **Implementation Guide** (setup & customization instructions)

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Import the Google Sheets template into your Google Drive
2. Follow the Implementation Guide to customize colors and add your restaurant info
3. Share the link with your management team
4. Print PDF versions for kitchen/front-of-house staff

## ⚙️ Key Automation Features

- **Labor Cost Calculation**: Auto-computes labor % and flags when exceeding 30% threshold
- **Overtime Alerts**: Warns when employees exceed 40 hours/week
- **Reorder Automation**: Auto-generates reorder list when inventory hits 30% par level
- **Clopen Detection**: Alerts for close-to-open shift violations
- **Recurring Task Population**: Automatically generates weekly/daily tasks
- **Failed Item Escalation**: Auto-adds incomplete opening/closing items to manager tasks
- **Compliance Integration**: Links failed safety checks to audit log for tracking
